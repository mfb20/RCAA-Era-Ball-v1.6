RCAA ERA BALL · HOW TO PLAY

THE FORMAT

SEVEN WAYS TO BUILD. ONE RCAA BOWL.

  1. Standard: four random team rolls, one player from each.
  2. RCAA IQ: Standard rules, but ratings, positions, awards, and bios are hidden until you draft the player. Names, teams, and PFPs stay visible.
  3. Bench: five rolls, then choose four starters on each side.
  4. Fantasy: a four-team, seven-round snake draft using every historical player instance.
  5. Multiplayer: host/join a 2-player synchronized 1v1 or a 4-player seven-round fantasy lobby.
  6. Sandbox: browse any season and team directly and choose any four player versions.
  7. Nightmare: no rerolls, elite roll teams removed, and your Bowl opponent is TEAM GOAT — the strongest legal roster the database can produce.
Lineup rules remain 1 QB + 3 WR on offense and 1 RUSH + 3 CB on defense. QB→WR: −5%. WR→QB: −50%. RUSH↔CB: −8%. Award and championship traits still affect simulation.

Standard, RCAA IQ, and Bench receive one Era + Team reroll and one Team reroll. Regular seasons use two Bad, two OK, and two Good historical teams. Semifinals use Good teams. Standard/RCAA IQ/Bench/Sandbox Bowls use Legendary teams. Nightmare ends against TEAM GOAT. Click any regular-season result to inspect that game's full player stats.
